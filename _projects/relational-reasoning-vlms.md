This project studies analogical and relational reasoning in vision-language models. A key challenge is moving from single relations to **chains of relations**: for example, one entity may be under a second entity, which is itself next to a third.

My work includes constructing and filtering controlled visual stimuli, defining relation chains, and using **function-vector style analysis** to identify small sets of attention heads that are particularly representative of specific relations. The broader goal is to test whether a model encodes reusable relational structure or merely exploits local visual and linguistic regularities.

The project connects my interests in cognitive accounts of analogy with mechanistic questions about multimodal models.
