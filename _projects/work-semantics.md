For **UCLA Undergraduate Research Week** with Prof. Sam Cumming, I constructed a 23M-character diachronic corpus, trained period-specific Word2Vec models, aligned them with Orthogonal Procrustes, and engineered semantic axes to trace pejorative, urban-coding, and cultural-identity trajectories across historical periods.
