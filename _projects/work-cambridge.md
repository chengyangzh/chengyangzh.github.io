Working with **Prof. Weiwei Sun** on category-based computational reconstruction of historical Chinese sound systems. The project combines distinctive features, modern dialect reflexes, fanqie, rhyme-table structure, and cross-period constraints, and extends the historical representation with additional time planes reconstructed from philological evidence.
