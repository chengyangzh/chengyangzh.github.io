I work on questions where a language model's probability distribution can be compared with human linguistic expectations. Current directions include surprisal around verbs, objects, and adverbial positions in filler–gap materials, plausibility effects in long-distance dependencies, and multilingual animacy representation.

The point is not only to ask whether a model predicts the next word correctly, but whether its probability shifts line up with the structural expectations that linguistic theories and human experiments predict.
