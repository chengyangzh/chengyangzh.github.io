At the **Peking University School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences**, I designed a high-sensitivity 3D Spatial Reproduction Paradigm and conducted behavioral and VR experiments on interactions between temporal and spatial magnitude perception. I also worked on cross-linguistic gesture analysis across typologically distinct languages.
