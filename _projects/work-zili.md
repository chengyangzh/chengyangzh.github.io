At the **UCLA Perceptual Processing & Computational Lab**, I work across stereokinetic depth perception and face privacy. I implemented real-time hand-pose acquisition with SteamVR/OpenXR and Unity/C#, contributed to human–model privacy evaluation across 272 participants and five recognition systems, and am implementing diffusion-based adversarial makeup.
