About

02

I like research questions that begin with a strange human phenomenon and end with a computational claim precise enough to fail.

My work moves between language, perception, and machine intelligence. Across projects, I keep returning to the same question: what kinds of structure do intelligent systems discover, and what does their success or failure tell us about representation?

That has taken me from historical Chinese phonology and multilingual semantics to VR psychophysics, LLM surprisal, face privacy, and agent evaluation.
