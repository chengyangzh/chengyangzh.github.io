SIDE B / OPEN QUESTIONS

Things I keep thinking about

01

Can a model discover a useful representation before we name it?

Especially when humans rely on partial cues, category structure, or tacit conventions instead of a clean label space.

02

What should count as emergence?

Not “the loss went down,” but a behavior or internal structure that appears only when a system crosses a meaningful constraint.

03

Where do human shortcuts beat scale?

Historical reconstruction, geolocation, language learning, and other problems where the right prior can be worth more than another million examples.
