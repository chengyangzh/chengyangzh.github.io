selected research
2026 —

Philologist-inspired phonological reconstruction

University of Cambridge · Prof. Weiwei Sun

Developing category-based computational models for Middle Chinese–Old Mandarin reconstruction using distinctive features, modern dialect reflexes, fanqie, rhyme-table structure, and historical sound-change constraints.

2026 —

Language-model expectations in filler–gap dependencies

UCLA Language Processing Lab · Prof. Jesse Harris

Comparing human processing with GPT-2/HuggingFace surprisal at critical verb, object, and adverb regions to study how plausibility interacts with long-distance syntactic expectations.

2025 —

Perception, privacy, and model behavior

UCLA Perceptual Processing & Computational Lab

Building VR psychophysics experiments for stereokinetic depth perception and evaluating how face-privacy transformations affect humans and recognition systems differently.
