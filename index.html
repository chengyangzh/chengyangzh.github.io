Kt/V Adequacy Model

clinical ML · in progress

A hybrid model predicting and optimizing hemodialysis adequacy (Kt/V) from patient demographics and clinical indicators, with a second sub-model for convective/middle-molecule clearance. Built on a decade-plus longitudinal dataset from a hospital data partnership. My first real ML portfolio piece — gradient-boosted trees plus a deep learning component.

▲

EWCB

computational linguistics · research

Entrenchment-Weighted Compression Bottleneck — a module aimed at explaining grammaticalization unidirectionality through neural iterated learning. Original research sitting at the intersection of classical linguistic theory and AI, aimed at ACL/EMNLP.

●

顏師古音注 Data

digital humanities · phonology

Cleaning and structuring phonological annotations — fanqie, 讀如, 讀曰, direct homophone glosses — from Yan Shigu's commentary on the Han Shu, plus the Hongwu Zhengyun. The cleaned data now feeds a Middle Chinese consonant reconstruction model built on mixed integer optimization.

■

Cantonese & Southern Min

historical linguistics · collaboration

Working with Prof. Weiwei Sun (Cambridge) on Cantonese syntax and Southern Min phonology, drawing on classical Chinese phonology sources — Baxter & Sagart, 王力, 潘悟云, 鄭張尚芳.
