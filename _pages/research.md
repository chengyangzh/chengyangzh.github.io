My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational linguistics, and cognitive science.

## Analogical and relational reasoning

I am currently working on analogical and relational reasoning in vision-language models, including controlled visual relation chains and function-vector-style analyses of relation-specific computation.

## Computational historical phonology

With Prof. Weiwei Sun, I work on category-based computational reconstruction of historical Chinese phonology, using structured phonological representations and evidence across historical periods and modern reflexes.

## Human perception and AI

At UCLA, I have worked on stereokinetic depth perception in VR and on comparisons between human and machine face recognition under privacy-preserving distortions.

## Language models and linguistic structure

I have also worked on language-model surprisal, filler-gap dependencies, animacy, and multilingual linguistic structure.
