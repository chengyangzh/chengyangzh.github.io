I am an undergraduate at **UCLA** studying Cognitive Science with a Computing specialization and Linguistics & Computer Science. I am broadly interested in artificial intelligence, computational linguistics, and cognitive science, especially how intelligent systems learn and reason over structure in language, perception, and experience.

My current work includes relational and analogical reasoning in vision-language models, computational historical phonology, language-model evaluation, virtual-reality perception experiments, and human–AI comparisons.
