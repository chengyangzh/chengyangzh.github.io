I am an undergraduate at **UCLA** pursuing Cognitive Science with a Computing specialization and Linguistics & Computer Science. I am broadly interested in how intelligent systems learn structure from language, perception, and experience, especially when human cognition gives us a sharper way to evaluate artificial systems.

My current work spans computational reconstruction of historical sound systems, language-model expectations in sentence processing, human–AI differences in perception and face privacy, and agent systems.
