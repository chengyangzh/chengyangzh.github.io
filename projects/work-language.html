UCLA · Language Processing

Human sentence processing and language-model surprisal in filler–gap dependencies.

Research Assistant in the UCLA Language Processing Lab with Prof. Jesse Harris. I am comparing human processing patterns with GPT-2/HuggingFace surprisal at critical verb, object, and adverb regions to study how semantic plausibility interacts with long-distance syntactic expectations.
