Face-privacy methods are often evaluated against recognition models, but the human side of the problem can behave differently. In this project, we compare how privacy-preserving distortions affect people and modern face-recognition systems.

The human study collected 108,800 recognition judgments from 272 participants. Model evaluation spans AdaFace, ArcFace, FaceNet512, SFace, and VGG-Face. The emerging pattern is that distortions can impair human recognition substantially while producing smaller or qualitatively different changes in machine performance.

I am also extending the evaluation beyond earlier adversarial approaches toward diffusion-based privacy methods and cross-model transfer.
